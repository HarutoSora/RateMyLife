Rate My Life — Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: August 2026

Rate My Life is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users. We have a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

These Child Safety Standards explain the types of conduct and content that are prohibited on Rate My Life and how users can report suspected violations.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Rate My Life strictly prohibits any content, behavior, or activity that involves the sexual abuse, exploitation, or endangerment of children.

Users may not use Rate My Life to:

  • Create, upload, request, share, distribute, or promote CSAM.
  • Sexualize, exploit, or abuse minors.
  • Engage in sexual communication with minors.
  • Groom or attempt to groom a minor.
  • Solicit sexual images or sexual content from minors.
  • Encourage or facilitate sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Promote or facilitate child trafficking or sexual exploitation.
  • Attempt to arrange sexual contact with a minor.
  • Use the platform to facilitate any other form of child sexual exploitation or abuse.

Any such activity is strictly prohibited regardless of whether the content is real, altered, generated, or otherwise manipulated.

2. User Reporting

Users can report content or behavior that they believe involves child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Reports can be submitted through the reporting functionality available within the Rate My Life application.

Users can also contact our safety team directly:

When submitting a report, users should provide as much relevant information as possible, including:

  • The username or profile involved
  • A description of the suspected violation
  • The location of the content within the application
  • Any relevant report or content ID

Do not intentionally download, copy, or redistribute suspected illegal material when making a report.

3. Enforcement

Rate My Life takes reports involving child safety seriously.

When we identify or receive credible reports of prohibited activity, we may take appropriate action, including:

  • Removing prohibited content.
  • Restricting or disabling accounts.
  • Permanently terminating accounts.
  • Preventing users from creating new accounts where appropriate.
  • Preserving relevant information when legally appropriate.
  • Escalating serious incidents to appropriate authorities when required by applicable law.

We may take immediate action when necessary to protect users or prevent further abuse.

4. Cooperation With Authorities

Rate My Life will cooperate with appropriate law enforcement and relevant authorities regarding suspected child sexual abuse and exploitation when required or permitted by applicable law.

Where legally required, we may preserve and provide relevant information to authorized authorities.

5. Prohibited Attempts to Circumvent Safety Measures

Users may not attempt to circumvent Rate My Life's safety systems, reporting mechanisms, account restrictions, or enforcement actions in order to facilitate prohibited activity.

Attempts to evade enforcement may result in additional account restrictions or permanent account termination.

6. Age and User Safety

Rate My Life is intended for users who meet the minimum age requirements specified by the application and applicable laws.

Users must provide accurate information about their age where required.

Any attempt to use Rate My Life to exploit, groom, sexually solicit, or otherwise endanger a minor is prohibited.

7. Our Commitment

Rate My Life is committed to:

  • Maintaining a zero-tolerance approach toward CSAE and CSAM.
  • Providing mechanisms for users to report suspected violations.
  • Reviewing safety reports appropriately.
  • Taking enforcement action against violations.
  • Cooperating with appropriate authorities when required.
  • Continuously improving our safety and moderation practices.

These standards apply to all users and all content and activity on Rate My Life.
